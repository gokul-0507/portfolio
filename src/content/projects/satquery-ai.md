## Overview

SatQuery AI is an agentic satellite imagery assistant. The system focuses on interpreting earth observation data using natural language queries and autonomous tool execution.

## Problem

Interpreting multi-spectral satellite imagery typically requires specialized domain expertise, manual dataset querying, and knowledge of complex geospatial processing pipelines. Non-specialists face steep barriers when seeking rapid insights from earth observation feeds.

## Approach & Architecture

The architecture models query interpretation as an agentic pipeline:

1. **User Query**: Natural language question regarding a region of interest or land-use change.
2. **Reasoning Agent**: Deconstructs query intent, selects spatial coordinates, and determines required spectral bands.
3. **Imagery Retrieval & Processing**: Queries satellite data interfaces and runs preprocessing algorithms.
4. **Synthesized Response**: Produces visual highlights and grounded analytical explanations.
